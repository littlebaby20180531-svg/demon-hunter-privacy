This policy explains how information is handled when you use our game and contact us. The information processed depends on the game version and the optional services you use.

1. Gameplay and local storage

The core single-player game uses local storage for progress, inventory and settings. These records remain on your device unless you choose a platform backup or another supported external service. Clearing app data or uninstalling may delete local progress. Platform backups, if enabled, are controlled by your device and Google settings.

Request data deletion — Demon Hunter: Dungeon

  1. Email littlebaby20180531@gmail.com with the subject “Demon Hunter data deletion”.
  2. Describe the support messages or other information held by verygoodgames that you want deleted. We will review your request and delete information we hold unless retention is required by law or to resolve a transaction or dispute. If an exception applies, we will explain the data and retention period in our reply. Do not send passwords or payment-card details.
  3. For local progress and settings, use your device's app storage controls to clear this game's data. This is permanent and does not issue a refund. Google Play purchase records and Unity service records are controlled by those providers; use their privacy request channels linked below for data they retain. Clearing local data does not delete a device or Google backup.

The game does not create a separate game account. This request process also applies without a game account.

2. Purchases and platform services

If you make an in-app purchase, Google Play and the purchasing service process the product, transaction or purchase token, purchase status and technical information needed to complete, validate and restore purchases and diagnose failures. We use purchase results to deliver game content and prevent duplicate delivery. We do not receive or store your payment-card number or Google password. Google and Unity may process product and purchase events, session or installation identifiers where available, app and operating-system versions, device model, network information and diagnostics to operate, measure and protect purchasing services under their own policies. Requests made by this Android version use encrypted transport. Ordinary offline progress and inventory are not uploaded by our game.

3. Other services and older versions

The revived Android version 8.0 does not enable in-game advertising, community login or a developer-run game account. Google Play purchases use your platform account. The game reads the device language locally on first launch and stores a language you manually choose; this does not require access to your contacts, phone number or location.

Some older releases or optional features may use third-party community, analytics or advertising services. Where present and enabled, these providers may process device identifiers, network information, usage events or advertising interactions under their own policies. Sharing or signing in through a third-party service is handled by that service. We do not ask you to send us your social-account password, and do not store social-account passwords. Check the services available in your installed version and the corresponding provider disclosures.

4. Support and use of information

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to include. We use it to answer your request, investigate problems and handle privacy requests. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or unnecessary identity documents. We do not sell your personal information to advertisers or other operators. Information may be disclosed where required by law or needed to protect users and the service.

5. Retention, security and your choices

Local game records are kept until you remove them or the app's data. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve requests and meet applicable obligations. Purchase and platform records may be retained by Google or other providers under their own retention rules. Reasonable safeguards are used, but no storage or transmission system is completely secure. To request access, correction or deletion of information held by us, contact us using the address below. Deleting local game data does not automatically delete platform purchase records or issue refunds.

6. Children

We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children contrary to applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can investigate and take appropriate action. Parents can manage purchases and platform permissions using Google Play and device controls.

7. Third-party policies

Third-party services operate under their own terms and privacy policies. This policy does not replace them.

8. Changes and contact
